How to Build a List That Doesn't Burn You Out

September 2026

The biggest way people quit a 31-for-31 list around movie 12 is front-loading it with three-hour A24 dread and nothing else. By day 10 you're exhausted and the challenge feels like homework. The fix is pacing, not willpower.

Mix your registers. Alternate a slow-burn (The Witch, Hereditary) with something fast and campy (Malignant, Studio 666) every few nights. Save your heaviest, most demanding picks for weekends when you have the mental bandwidth, and keep weeknights lighter — a solid slasher runs 90 minutes and asks nothing of you emotionally.

Leave a few slots open. Don't lock all 31 in September — leave 4 or 5 blank so you can slot in whatever your friends are raving about mid-month, or a rewatch you're suddenly craving.

And track it as you go, not after. Marking movies watched and jotting a quick note the same night keeps the challenge feeling alive instead of becoming a chore you're behind on by week two — that's exactly what My Lists is for.
