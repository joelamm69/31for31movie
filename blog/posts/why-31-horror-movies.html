Why 31 Horror Movies in 31 Days?

September 2026

October gives you exactly the right number of nights for exactly the wrong kind of movie. One horror film a night for 31 nights isn't just a gimmick — it turns the whole month into a slow, deliberate binge instead of a single exhausting weekend where everything blurs together by the third film.

Pacing matters. A single sitting rarely lets a movie's dread actually settle before the next one wipes it out. Spread the same 31 movies across 31 days and each one gets to live in your head for a full day before the next one shows up. That's the whole point of the challenge: not just watching more horror, but watching it in a way that actually lands.

It also turns into something social. Compare lists with friends, argue about whether Hereditary counts as a slow burn or just slow, and by Halloween you've got a list — and a body count — worth bragging about.
