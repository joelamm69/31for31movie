Terms of Use

Last updated November 2026

These terms cover this website and the 31 for 31 app, which share one account. By creating a profile, posting notes or reviews, or following people, you agree to them.

No tolerance for abuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't post anything that:

  • harasses, threatens, bullies or targets another person;
  • is hateful toward people based on who they are;
  • is sexually explicit, or sexualizes minors in any way;
  • is spam, advertising, or impersonates someone else;
  • spoils a movie or episode without being marked as a spoiler.

Horror is the point here, so talking about violent or disturbing films is fine. Directing that at real people is not.

Reporting and blocking

Every note, review, profile and shared challenge has a Report option, and you can Block anyone. Blocking hides each of you from the other and removes follows between you. Reports go to the moderators, who review them within 24 hours and remove content and ban accounts that break these terms.

Your content

You own what you write. You choose who sees each note or review: everyone, your followers, or only you. By posting something public or followers-only you let us show it to those people on the site and in the app. You can edit or delete it any time.

Filtering

Strong language in notes and reviews is masked by default. You can turn this off in your profile settings.

Movie and TV data

Titles, posters and descriptions come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Accounts

You must be at least 13 to use 31 for 31. We may suspend accounts that break these terms. To delete your account, email us.
