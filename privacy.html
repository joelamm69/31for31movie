Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

31for31 ("the app") is made by Joe Lamm ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. It also covers this website, which uses the same account and data as the app.

What the app collects

  • Account details: your email address, your password (stored only in hashed form by my sign-in provider), the display name you choose, and basic account records such as when you signed up and whether your email is verified.
  • What you add in the app: star ratings, movies you log as watched, lists you publish, and your personal library (your own lists, watched movies and notes on movies).
  • Technical data: when the app talks to my servers, the hosting provider keeps standard request logs, which can include your IP address, device and app details, and the time of the request.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or payment details. It currently shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites. If that changes, I'll update this policy before the change takes effect.

This website

When you sign in here, you use the same account as the app, and your lists, ratings and notes are shared between the two. The site uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If you sign up for the newsletter on the home page, your email address is stored for the newsletter; email me to be removed.

How I use it

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To save your library and sync it across your devices.
  • To show community features: ratings, watch activity and published lists.
  • To keep the service secure and answer support requests.

I don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

What other people can see

Your display name is shown next to your ratings, your watch activity (for example, that you watched a movie) and any list you publish. These are visible to anyone using the app. Your email address, private library and movie notes are visible only to you.

Where your data is stored

  • Supabase provides the database, sign-in and hosting. Data is stored on servers in the United States. Supabase processes it only on my behalf, to provide that service.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) supplies movie details and posters. When the app loads them, TMDB receives an ordinary web request from your device, including its IP address, but not your account details. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Apple handles downloads and, if you choose to share them, crash reports.

When data is shared

I share data only with the providers above so the app can work, when the law requires it, to protect the safety or rights of users or others, or if the app is transferred to a new owner who agrees to this policy.

How long it's kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and everything tied to it (ratings, watch activity, published lists, library and notes) is removed from the live database right away. The hosting provider's request logs are kept for a short time, currently 1 day, and then deleted automatically. The database currently has no automatic backups. If I turn backups on later, deleted data could stay in them until they expire, and I'll update this policy first.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app at the bottom of the Account page (tap Delete Account). You can also remove individual ratings and published lists in the app, or email me at jlamm6@me.com and I'll delete your data.

Children

The app is about horror films and isn't meant for children under 13. I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has created an account, email me and I'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it's used. Email me to make a request and I'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules let only you change your own data. No system is perfectly secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, including before adding ads, analytics or in-app purchases, I'll update the effective date above, and I'll tell you in the app before any major change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests: jlamm6@me.com
